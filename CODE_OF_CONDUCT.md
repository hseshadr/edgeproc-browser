# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.
Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning behaviour are not welcome here, in
issues, pull requests, or anywhere else the project happens.

Report a problem to `harish.seshadri@gmail.com`. Reports are handled privately.
The maintainers may warn, block, or ban anyone whose behaviour is out of line,
and will not discuss individual reports in public.
